In-store category / department sign — a big friendly color-coded label with a soft blob accent and icon tile.

```jsx
<CategorySign title="Одяг" subtitle="0–12 років" color="blue" icon={<ShirtIcon/>} />
<CategorySign title="Іграшки" color="green" icon={<BlocksIcon/>} />
```

`color`: `pink | blue | green | yellow` — use the accent set to color-code departments consistently across a store.
