Playful sale sticker for posters and shelf edges — soft "blob" or circle with a white keyline.

```jsx
<SaleBurst top="знижка" main="−50%" color="pink" size={160} />
<SaleBurst main="АКЦІЯ" sub="до неділі" color="blue" shape="circle" />
```

`color`: `pink | blue | green | yellow`. `shape`: `blob (default) | circle`. `size` is the diameter in px; inner type scales with it.
