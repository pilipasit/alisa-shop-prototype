Soft rounded surface container for product cards, panels and info blocks.

```jsx
<Card hover padding="none">
  <img src="toy.jpg" />
  <div style={{padding:'16px'}}>…</div>
</Card>
<Card padding="lg">Звичайний блок вмісту</Card>
```

`padding`: `none | sm | md | lg`. `hover` adds a lift for clickable cards; `flat` removes the shadow.
