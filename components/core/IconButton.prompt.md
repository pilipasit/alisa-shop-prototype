Circular icon-only button — favorite, cart, close, toolbar actions. Always pass `aria-label`.

```jsx
<IconButton aria-label="У бажане" variant="soft"><HeartIcon/></IconButton>
<IconButton aria-label="Закрити" variant="ghost"><XIcon/></IconButton>
```

Variants: `solid | soft | ghost (default) | outline`. Sizes `sm | md | lg` (44px md meets touch-target minimum).
