# Social — UI Kit

Square 1080×1080 posts for Instagram / Facebook, built from the design system.

- **SocialPost.jsx** — one component, three `kind`s:
  - `sale` — bold pink background, big "РОЗПРОДАЖ", yellow sale burst, price chip.
  - `arrival` — soft blue, new-collection headline, product photo slot.
  - `announce` — cream, centered store announcement with CTA.
- **index.html** — the three posts side by side (rendered at 0.42 scale for preview).

Swap the "Фото товару" placeholder for real product photography. Keep headlines short and in Fredoka; body in Nunito.
